## Brand Essence

Coates Media is a **tech-and-marketing studio** for service-based businesses. Every discipline that touches a client's marketing — strategy, brand, design, web, custom apps, CRM, and the automation behind it — handled end to end, in one studio. Clients hire Coates Media when they need a real marketing function but don't want to coordinate five vendors.

The brand is a balance of **simple elegance and liveliness** (carried forward from the 2018 brand guide) — refined, modern, and warm rather than corporate or stiff.

The original 2018 framing — "discover your brand" — still works as an underlying ethos. Coates Media helps service-based businesses figure out who they are operationally, technically, and visually, and then builds the infrastructure to express it.

> **Internal note (don't surface in external copy):** Coates Media is currently founded and operated by Alexis ("Lexi") Coates, who personally blends creative and technical work — design, code, strategy, brand, ops. External-facing copy doesn't lead with headcount. Don't fabricate a team that doesn't exist, but don't actively flag the company as solo either — frame around capability and integration. A team may exist in the future; the copy should age either way.

## Voice & Tone

### Voice (consistent — how Coates Media sounds always)

- **Chill and friendly.** Warm conversational tone. Not corporate, not cold, not overly formal.
- **Confident and technical.** Knows what she's talking about. Doesn't apologize for technical depth.
- **Direct.** Says what she does and doesn't do. No agency-speak, no fluff.
- **Smart but not pretentious.** Plain language for complex concepts. Short sentences. Owns expertise without flexing.
- **Problem-solving energy.** "Everyone else told you it's not possible — let me figure it out." That's the real value prop. Not "we have a process for everything"; more like "we'll figure it out, that's literally the job."
- **Wears many hats** as a feature, not a limitation. Lean into being a full-stack operator who can do design *and* code *and* strategy.

### Tone (varies by context)

| Context | Tone shift |
|---|---|
| Homepage / first-touch | Direct, confident, slightly playful — "this is what we do, here's why we're different" |
| Services page | Specific, concrete, technical-but-readable. List actual things, not "transformative solutions" |
| Case studies / portfolio | Honest about what we did, what worked, what didn't. Skip the agency-speak success-story tone |
| Proposals to potential clients | Warm, direct, structured. Show the path, name the tradeoffs |
| Blog / writing | Educational, opinionated, technical. The "I figured this out so you don't have to" voice |
| Social media | Casual, real. Behind-the-scenes work, technical curiosities, occasional client wins (with permission) |
| Internal client comms | Warm, direct, structured. No surprise problems — flag things early |
| Legal pages (privacy, ToS, data deletion) | Plain language but precise. Required to be technically accurate; otherwise stay readable. |

## Visual Identity — Colors

Per the 2018 official brand guide, locked.

### Primary Colors

| Color | Hex | RGB | CMYK | Pantone | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Royal Marine** | `#093142` | 9, 49, 66 | 95, 71, 51, 50 | 547C | Primary dark navy — backgrounds, header type, deep contrast partner. Used in logo bottom half. |
| **Teal Tale** | `#5BB4AC` | 91, 180, 172 | 62, 9, 37, 0 | 7472C | Primary teal — backgrounds, headers, sub-headers, gradient overlays with white. Used in logo top half. The accent color. |

> **Note:** the 2018 brand guide originally had these names swapped (Royal Marine labeled with the teal hex, Teal Tale labeled with the navy hex). Names corrected 2026-05-08 so they match the colors intuitively — Royal Marine = the deep navy, Teal Tale = the teal. Hex codes are unchanged from the original spec; only the name-to-color mapping was fixed.

### Secondary Colors (grays for body, backgrounds, supporting copy)

| Color | Hex | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Grandpa Gray** | `#DCDCDC` | 220, 220, 220 | Light gray. Background fills, body copy on dark backgrounds. (Per guide: "always used as body copy font color.") |
| **Gray** | `#999999` | 153, 153, 153 | Mid gray. Subheader type. |
| **Grandson Gray** | `#333333` | 51, 51, 51 | Dark gray. Backgrounds, dark text on light. Body copy alternative on light backgrounds. |

### Color rules

- **Always include the two primary colors** in any design — they are the brand identity.
- **Use grays sparingly** as compliments, not as primary brand colors.
- **Teal Tale + white gradient overlays** for imagery — official treatment (this is the lighter, more vibrant primary).
- **Don't pair dark grays with Royal Marine** without intent — legibility first.

## Visual Identity — Fonts

Per the 2018 official brand guide.

| Font | Use |
|---|---|
| **Bebas Neue** | Headlines, all-caps. The "brazen sans-serif" attention-grabber. |
| **Kepler Std** | Subheaders. Serif for professionalism, distinct color from body. |
| **Rucksack** | Body text. The "playful book sans-serif" that balances the rigid headline + serif subheader. Generally black. |
| **Hucklebuck JF** | Decorative script. **Limited use only** — special marketing campaigns, accent moments. Adds gaiety; never use for body or whole sentences. |

### Font usage rules

- Headlines: Bebas Neue, all caps, generous letter-spacing
- Subheads: Kepler Std, mixed case, in a primary color (Royal Marine or Teal Tale typically)
- Body: Rucksack, mixed case, in Grandpa Gray on dark backgrounds or Grandson Gray on light
- Decorative phrase: Hucklebuck JF, sparingly, never all-caps

### Web font availability

For website builds, Bebas Neue is freely available via Google Fonts. Kepler Std is Adobe Fonts (license required). Rucksack and Hucklebuck JF are commercial — confirm licensing before web embedding. **[FILL: confirm web licensing for Kepler Std, Rucksack, Hucklebuck JF]** or substitute with closest free alternatives if licensing is a blocker.

## Visual Identity — Logo

The Coates logo is **"coates" in script type, enclosed in a duo-tone circle** — Royal Marine top, Teal Tale bottom.

### Logo rules (per 2018 guide)

- **Always displayed at a 4.5° angle.** Never straight.
- **Minimum size:** 1 inch × 1 inch.
- **Don't alter the logo in any way.** No skewing, distorting, color changes, or typography changes.
- **One logo per design piece.** Never combine multiple logo variants on the same surface.

### Logo files

Available at `/Users/ancoates/Documents/Business/Coates Media/Branding/Logo/`:

- `ColoredLogo.ai` / `ColoredLogo.png` — primary (Royal Marine + Teal Tale)
- `darklogo.ai` — for dark backgrounds (use Royal Marine and white)
- `lightlogo.ai` — for white backgrounds (uses dark navy + teal)
- `lightgraylogo.ai` / `WHITEGRAYLOGO.ai` / `WHITEGRAYLOGO.png` — gray variants
- `BWLogo.ai` — black and white only, for B&W print
- `fblogo.ai` / `iglogo.ai` / `twitterlogo.ai` — sized for social profiles
- `favicon.ai` / `favicon.png` — for browser tabs / web favicons
- `smalllogo.ai` — small-size optimized

### Approved logo color combinations (per 2018 guide)

1. **B&W variant** — white backgrounds only, primarily for B&W print
2. **Teal Tale + Royal Marine** — dark backgrounds (Royal Marine or black). Never on white.
3. **Teal-tinted variant** — never on white or light backgrounds. Use with Teal Tale or black backgrounds.
4. **White + Royal Marine** — light backgrounds. Never on dark, for legibility.

## Things to Avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| Agency-speak ("synergy," "leverage," "transform your business") | Off-brand; reads as fake. Coates Media is direct and concrete. |
| Generic "we help businesses succeed" copy | Says nothing. Be specific about what we actually do. |
| Pretending to be a 50-person agency you're not | Don't fabricate team size or capacity. |
| Leading with headcount in customer-facing copy | Don't actively flag the studio as solo either. Frame around capability and integration — "the whole marketing stack in one studio" reads correctly whether the team grows or stays as it is. |
| Over-promising on timelines | Be honest. Real estimates with tradeoffs explained. |
| Claiming AI does the work | AI is a tool Coates Media uses. The judgment, design, strategy, and ownership are Lexi's. |
| Apologizing for being technical | The technical depth is a value prop, not a liability. |
| Treating clients as if they're not technical | Our default is "you're smart, here's the actual answer." Adjust if a specific client signals otherwise. |
| Cliché design (stock photos of handshakes, generic charts, "creative" flat illustrations) | Coates has a specific visual language — stick to it. |
| Tagline-style fluff like "passionate about helping you succeed" | Doesn't say anything; lowers the brand. |
| Mixing the logo with other organization logos | Logo rules forbid this. |
| Altering logo colors, type, angle, or position | All locked per the brand guide. |
| Putting Hucklebuck JF anywhere it doesn't belong | Decorative use only; never body, never headlines, never all-caps. |

## Ideal AI Behavior

When generating content for Coates Media (website copy, proposals, blog posts, ad copy, social, email, anything customer-facing), AI should:

- **Default to direct, technical, warm.** No agency-speak. No fluff.
- **Treat readers as smart.** Plain language for complex things; don't dumb down.
- **Respect the visual identity.** Color, font, and logo rules are locked. Don't suggest swapping primary colors or using non-brand fonts unless explicitly asked.
- **Surface the integration angle** when relevant. Coates Media's whole differentiator is handling every discipline that touches a client's marketing — in one studio, with nothing handed off between vendors. The "wears many hats" framing still works conceptually; don't translate that into "one person" or "sole operator" in customer-facing copy.
- **Don't fabricate team size or capacity.** Also don't lead with headcount. The factual operator/founder situation lives in this brand doc; external copy frames around capability and integration so the messaging stays correct if the team grows later.
- **Use real client names** only when they appear in `schema.md`'s Public Clients list. Confidential clients should not be referenced even if useful for context.
- **For client work executed under Coates Media** (e.g., FADS work), defer to that client's brand.md — don't apply Coates Media voice to client-facing FADS material.

When in doubt, default to: **direct, warm, technical, honest, problem-solving, capability-confident.**
